# Mind Mingle

### About

Mind Mingle is a social platform designed to support individuals struggling with mental health. It's a safe space to remind users that they are not alone, even when faced with insensitivity from others. On Mind Mingle, users can share posts, resources, and expertise, send direct messages, create peer groups, and interact with a fake AI therapist.

In the future, the platform will offer journaling tools, event creation, and meetups. Mental health professionals are welcome to join, but must identify themselves. Mind Mingle is not a substitute for therapy, but it will provide resources to help users find the support they need.

While making friends is not the primary goal, it’s an option. Ethical standards of conduct apply, and users who violate the terms and conditions may be banned, with the severity determining the length of the ban. Additionally, users can apply to become moderators to help maintain a welcoming and kind environment.

At its core, Mind Mingle is about kindness, connection, and overcoming adversity together.
